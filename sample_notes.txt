Cell biology (also cellular biology or cytology) is a branch of biology that studies the structure, function, and behavior of cells.
All living organisms are made of cells. A cell is the basic unit of life that is responsible for the living and functioning of organisms.
Eukaryotic cells have a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles, whereas prokaryotic cells do not have a nucleus.
Eukaryotic organisms can be unicellular or multicellular.
Mitochondria are known as the powerhouses of the cell. They generate ATP through cellular respiration.
Ribosomes are responsible for protein synthesis.
The cell membrane protects the cell and controls the movement of substances in and out of cells.
